Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Who we are

Two-Headed Commander (twoheadedcommander.com) is a fan-made, non-commercial website dedicated to a custom Magic: The Gathering format. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Wizards of the Coast.

2. What data we collect

We only collect data if you explicitly accept analytics cookies. When you do, Google Analytics 4 collects:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Browser type, operating system, and screen size
  • Approximate geographic location (country / city level — no precise location)
  • Referring website (how you found us)
  • Search terms entered in the banned-card search box

We do not collect names, email addresses, or any information that directly identifies you. IP addresses are anonymised by Google before storage.

3. Why we collect it

Analytics data helps us understand which pages are most useful, how visitors find the site, and how to improve the format documentation. No data is sold or shared for advertising purposes.

4. Legal basis (GDPR)

We rely on your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR) to process analytics data. You can withdraw consent at any time (see section 7).

5. Data retention

Google Analytics retains event-level data for 14 months (the shortest available setting). Aggregated, non-identifiable reports may be kept indefinitely.

6. Third parties

Analytics data is processed by Google LLC under Google's privacy framework. Google may transfer data to servers outside the EU/EEA under Standard Contractual Clauses. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

No other third-party services receive your data.

7. Your rights & withdrawing consent

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of data held about you. Because we collect no directly identifying information, most requests should be directed to Google via their data deletion tools.
